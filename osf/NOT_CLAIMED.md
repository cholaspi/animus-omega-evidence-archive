# Claims not made by the Animus-cycle specification

**Status:** companion claims boundary for the proposed design specification,
version 1.0, 2026-09-11.

The Animus-cycle specification and its frozen reference fixtures do not
establish:

- legal exclusivity over the words “Animus cycle”;
- legal exclusivity over the proposed design name “Animus Omega Cycle”;
- priority over checkpointing, event sourcing, deterministic replay,
  fixed-point constraints, self-consistency constraints, bounded-observer
  models, or state reconstruction;
- that the complete architecture is scientifically or technically novel;
- that naming the optional branch-reconvergence version “Animus Omega Cycle”
  establishes novelty, priority, implementation, or validation;
- that a documented negative systematic search establishes the complete
  conjunction is absent from prior systems;
- that Tests 01–04 establish an Animus cycle;
- a Test 05 result;
- physical recurrence or a cyclic physical universe;
- that spatial torus topology creates temporal branches or forces them to
  reconverge;
- that candidate enumeration, observer threads, an always-single trunk, branch
  deletion, or scripted equal outputs demonstrate represented branch
  reconvergence;
- that branch-set reduction by itself establishes preservation of protected
  semantics, deterministic replay, or reciprocal closure;
- that a successful branch-reconvergence fixture establishes that arbitrary
  branches can reconcile, that one history is physically real, or that physical
  alternatives cease to exist;
- that “narrative collapse” describes quantum measurement, physical realities,
  or the destruction of universes;
- consciousness, subjective continuity, or observer indistinguishability;
- unrestricted predestination or information arriving from an unconstrained
  future;
- entropy removal, information creation, or exact recovery after a lossy
  transformation;
- general computational, memory, energy, or resource advantage;
- that every ledger-free architecture must fail;
- that provenance labels or authored-at metadata prove ending authorship;
- that writer eligibility implies intention, awareness, or motive;
- that a protected ledger alone establishes continuity through information
  loss; or
- that a local draft has an OSF identifier, DOI, publication date, or
  Registration status.

The reference fixtures are executable teaching examples. Their PASS and FAIL
values are conditional on their frozen candidate domain, contracts,
reconstruction rules, observer model, interventions, and validator.

Any future reserved branch-reconvergence result is conditional on its
prospectively time-locked fixtures, branch schema, transition table,
narrative-equivalence projection, conflict rules, merge schedule, controls,
thresholds, exclusions, and failure rules. Changing any of those after
inspection of reserved outcomes creates a new protocol; it cannot rescue or
reinterpret the original run.

Tests 01–04 remain separate retrospective studies with their original scopes,
outcomes, deviations, and limitations. Test 03 remains unsupported. Test 04
Revision 1 remains aborted. Test 04 Revision 2 is limited to exact replay in its
fixed deterministic model.
